Beyond the Headline Issues

Where equality meets everyday life

LGBTQ+ Ohioans do not live single-issue lives. Healthcare, a paycheck, and a public school shape whether equality holds up in practice. A brief look at where each candidate stands; full platforms: Acton's · Ramaswamy's.

🩺Healthcare access

Care that is affordable and respectful decides whether equality on paper reaches the exam room.

Acton

Plans an “Ohio Rx” purchasing platform to drive down drug prices, plus a day-one push to relieve medical debt “for pennies on the dollar.” Acton platform

Ramaswamy

His principal published healthcare item is a Medicaid anti-fraud proposal; beyond it, his platform lists no other healthcare planks as of July 30, 2026. Vivek for Ohio · The Plan

💼Jobs and the economy

A paycheck and stable costs are the safety net LGBTQ+ Ohioans fall back on where legal protections are missing.

Acton

An affordability agenda built on a state child tax credit and Earned Income Credit, plus a property tax circuit breaker rebate of up to $1,000. Statehouse News Bureau, April 6, 2026 · July 20, 2026

Ramaswamy

Pledges to phase out the state income tax, “starting with eliminating capital gains taxes,” and to roll property taxes back to pre-pandemic levels. The Plan · Statehouse News Bureau, July 14, 2026

🏫Public schools

The bills in the table above land first in school buildings, and so does state funding policy.

Acton

Would restore the bipartisan Fair School Funding Plan, reform what she calls a “corrupt school voucher system,” and fund free school breakfast and lunch. Acton platform

Ramaswamy

An “Educational Achievement” pillar: raise academic standards, expand school choice, and tie teacher pay to merit. The Plan · WOSU, August 20, 2025

✊Reproductive freedom

Bodily autonomy is one fight; the same state offices that police reproductive care set the terms for trans healthcare.

Acton

“Every Ohioan deserves the right to make their own reproductive health care decisions, free from government interference, plain and simple.” Endorsed by Planned Parenthood Advocates of Ohio and Reproductive Freedom for All. Acton platform · PPAO, March 5, 2026

Ramaswamy

Called himself “unapologetically pro-life” in his 2023 presidential run; his 2026 platform does not mention abortion as of July 30, 2026. Heartland Signal, May 12, 2026

The Paths

Two roads to the Governor's Mansion

Both candidates were born in Ohio, made their names far outside elected office, and are now running for governor as first-time candidates for state office. Here's how each got here.

    The Job Itself

    Asked & Unanswered

    Ohio's governor signs or vetoes what the legislature passes, so Ohio Pride PAC is sending the same five questions to every candidate for governor, the same standard our scorecard applies to all 132 legislators of both parties. Answers and non-answers alike will be published here.

    1. Would you sign or veto HB 693, which shields parents who reject their child's gender identity?
    2. Would you sign a ban on gender-affirming care for adults if the legislature passed one?
    3. Do you support or oppose the Ohio Fairness Act?
    4. Would you sign or veto a statewide conversion-therapy ban?
    5. As governor, would you keep, expand, or roll back HB 68, SB 104, and HB 8?
    Status: questionnaire goes to every gubernatorial campaign in August 2026 — answers and non-answers will be published here.

    Our Standard

    How this guide was built

    This page grades no one. Ohio Pride PAC publishes a legislative scorecard that grades all 132 sitting Ohio legislators of both parties on their votes. Neither candidate for governor has a legislative voting record in Ohio, so there is nothing to grade — instead, this guide documents what both candidates have said and done, in their own words, with dates and links.

    • Every claim is sourced. If we can't link it, it isn't on this page.
    • Quotes are verbatim, with the venue and date they were said.
    • Silence is reported as silence — "no public position on record" — never interpreted as a position.
    • Statements by staff are attributed to staff, not to the candidate.
    • Corrections: spot an error? Tell us and we'll fix it and log the change here.
